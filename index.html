About Me

I am a Ph.D. research scholar in Digital Transformation and Business Intelligence at the School of Management and Entrepreneurship, Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur, working under the supervision of Dr. Deepak Kumar Saxena.

My research investigates how national AI policies translate from macro-level regulatory design to meso-level organizational governance and micro-level citizen and developer practices. Alongside academia, I bring over 13 years of operational and industrial experience in technology public policy, aviation maintenance engineering, and Network Centric Warfare systems.

I have presented research at national and international conferences hosted by institutions including BITS Pilani, IIM Indore, GIM Panjim, and NERIST Itanagar.

Research Direction

Key thematic pillars of my doctoral and computational policy work.

01 • Comparative Policy

An Indian Approach to AI Policy

Tracing historical policy trajectories across India's IT/ITES, Telecommunications, and Biotechnology sectors to inform a cohesive national AI regulatory framework.

02 • Computational Analysis

National AI Strategies & Topic Modelling

Bibliometric mapping and NLP-driven keyword and topic modelling across global national AI strategy documents to uncover governance paradigms.

03 • Policy to Practice

AI Governance & Citizen-Centric Systems

Multi-level empirical analysis of public e-governance applications and self-regulation dynamics in AI chatbots from both user and developer perspectives.

04 • Theoretical Frameworks

Established Policy Frameworks for AI

Applying foundational public policy theories—including sensemaking and multi-level translation—to emerging AI governance frontiers.
